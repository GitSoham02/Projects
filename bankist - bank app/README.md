# 📌 Bankist app

> A small scale Bank app showcasing real world operations like Login, Transactions, Taking loan,etc.
> It has 4 accounts which you can access by observing code. You can send money to other accounts and check if they recieved it.
> Recent statements can be viewed of each.
> Each account has income, expenditure and interest rate tab.
> You can also close account if you dont like this bank.
> You are automatically loged out after 5 mintues of inactivity.
